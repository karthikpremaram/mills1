Plastics Family Americas (PFA) is the industry’s largest wholesale, value-add
distributor of plastics. A privately held company with roots dating back
nearly 100 years, PFA comprises over 200 locations from more than 40
historical brands. Each location operates as an independent business unit,
focused on servicing local customers with a unique, decentralized, and
empowering culture.
